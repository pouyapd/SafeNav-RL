# Suggested reviewer expertise (areas only)

No reviewer names are proposed. If the journal asks for names, the author should choose them, check for conflicts of interest, and verify the current affiliations.

1. **Control barrier functions and safety filters.** Discrete-time CBFs; undesired equilibria and deadlock of CBF-based controllers.
2. **Safe reinforcement learning.** Shielding and safety filters during training; constrained policy optimisation; action projection and clipping.
3. **Mobile robot motion planning and navigation.** Local minima, recovery behaviours in navigation stacks, and global/local planning such as navigation functions and dynamic-window methods.
4. **Experimental methodology and statistics for RL and robotics.** Multi-seed evaluation, hierarchical bootstrap, pre-registration and multiplicity.
